About

I am an undergraduate student at the College of Software, Shanxi Agricultural University, on the Embedded Systems track. My research focuses on artificial intelligence and general large language models, with additional interests in neuroscience and interdisciplinary science. I am currently interested in model architectures, instruction tuning, and the construction and optimization of reasoning capabilities, as well as applied AI in vertical domains such as agriculture.

I am a member of the China Computer Federation (CCF). I presented my work as an Oral report at the 2026 6th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Big Data and Algorithms (CAIBDA), and I serve as a reviewer for Neural Computing and Applications.

Pioneering

Canbal v1.0 · Agricultural Intelligence System

A vertical-domain large language model system designed for smart agriculture. Built upon Qwen3-8B with extensive agricultural instruction tuning, the system integrates multi-source perception with intelligent decision-making. Pioneering the introduction of metacognitive mechanisms, the model achieves self-reflection and strategy optimization capabilities. It incorporates real-time information retrieval, meteorological data fusion, multi-agent collaborative reasoning, domain knowledge graph, and RAG-enhanced retrieval modules, providing comprehensive intelligent support for agricultural production, research, and management.

Highlights Real-time retrieval · Meteorological fusion · Multi-agent reasoning · Metacognitive self-reflection · Domain knowledge graph · RAG-enhanced retrieval
